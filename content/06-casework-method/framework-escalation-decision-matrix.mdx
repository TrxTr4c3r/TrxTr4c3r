# Escalation Decision Matrix and Triggers

<span style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--font-space-mono), monospace', fontSize: 13, opacity: 0.7 }}>`framework`</span>

## Core idea
Escalation fails in two directions: too late, after the money has moved past any practical preservation window, and too vague, arriving at the wrong desk with a package nobody can act on. Fix both by pre-defining the trigger, the destination and the contents. The trigger is a condition you check rather than a judgement you make under pressure, and the package is assembled before you need it.

## Components

**Seven triggers. Any one of them starts the clock.**
- Funds are actively moving, or have recently reached a hosted service.
- A centralised exchange, payment processor, fintech or crypto ATM operator may hold records.
- Ransomware, extortion, threats or public leak activity is involved.
- Sanctions, terrorist financing, child exploitation, narcotics or national-security indicators appear.
- Multiple victims or large aggregate losses are identified.
- Evidence is volatile: live domains, chats, dashboards, social channels.
- A potential insider or employee is implicated.

**Trigger to path to package.**

| Trigger | Escalate to | Package must include |
|---|---|---|
| Funds moving toward exchange exposure | Supervisor, counsel or compliance, exchange preservation contact, law enforcement where appropriate | Case ID, full transaction hashes, full addresses, chain, timestamps, loss amount, screenshots, urgency statement |
| Sanctions or terrorist-financing concern | Compliance leadership, sanctions team, FIU or law-enforcement channel, counsel | Screening source and date, wallet or service exposure, confidence level, limitations, related transactions |
| Ransomware or extortion deadline | Incident-response lead, law enforcement, insurer and counsel as applicable | Demand note, payment address, timeline, victim impact, communication evidence, preservation status |
| Multiple victims or an aggregation wallet | Fraud leadership, law enforcement referral, exchange and platform contacts | Victim list, aggregate loss, shared wallet evidence, convergence timeline, platform indicators |
| Potential insider or employee misconduct | Legal, HR or investigations leadership, access-control owner | Access logs, wallet evidence, policy basis, preservation requirements, confidentiality controls |

**Make the preservation request specific.** Name the records to preserve and the blockchain activity that justifies the request. Volatile categories matter most: login IPs, device fingerprints, communications, support tickets, internal risk notes. Where legal process is required for disclosure, preservation still holds the records in place while that process is prepared.

## When to use
At intake, to set the triggers for the case, and at every trace review afterwards. Check the triggers rather than recall them.

## Avoid when
Do not delay escalation until attribution is settled. Preservation and naming a suspect are separate steps with separate evidentiary bars, and the preservation window closes first.

## Example
A trace reaches an exchange-linked deposit address at 09:40. Trigger one is live, so the package goes to the supervisor and the exchange preservation contact the same morning with full hashes, chain, timestamps and an urgency statement. Account-holder identity is recorded as unknown pending records, which does not hold up the request.

# Pre-Incident Recovery Readiness

<span style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--font-space-mono), monospace', fontSize: 13, opacity: 0.7 }}>`checklist`</span>

## Core idea
Recovery outcomes are largely set before the theft. A holder who can produce an address inventory, an access log, and a pre-existing exchange contact recovers on a different timeline from one who cannot. For an analyst, this list doubles as an intake questionnaire: what exists here determines what the first 24 hours can realistically achieve.

## Components
**Asset mapping and documentation:**
- A secure off-chain record of every wallet address and its custody arrangement.
- Documented administrative access controls for exchange accounts, hot wallets, and cold storage.
- Established baseline transaction patterns, so an anomaly is recognisable as one.
- Regular backup procedures for key material and seed phrases, with the recovery mechanisms actually tested rather than assumed.

**Relationships established in advance:**
- Local law enforcement with genuine cyber capability.
- Regulators in the relevant jurisdictions.
- Key exchanges and VASPs, for expedited disclosure.
- Forensic blockchain analysis providers.
- Technical experts with court testimony experience.
- Local counsel in the common crypto-friendly jurisdictions.

**Legal templates ready to fire:**
- Ex parte freeze applications for the major jurisdictions.
- Third-party disclosure applications.
- Emergency arbitration requests.
- Criminal referral packages.
- Pre-drafted affidavits explaining blockchain mechanics to a non-technical court.

**Chain-specific preparation:**
- *Bitcoin:* real-time withdrawal alerts on material addresses, and documented coinbase-to-address maturity where relevant to temporal traceability.
- *Ethereum:* documented smart contract dependencies and admin keys, a current list of the wrapped asset contracts and bridges in use, and alerting on material gas spikes.
- *Stablecoins:* documented freeze and blacklist capability for each issuer used, direct legal escalation contacts at those issuers, and prepared declarations explaining stablecoin mechanics.

## When to use
At intake on any recovery matter, to establish what the client can actually hand over. Also when advising a holder or a business on readiness before anything has gone wrong.

## Avoid when
Do not treat a gap here as a reason to decline. Most victims have none of it. The value is in knowing which of the first 24 hours' actions are still available.

# Casework

<span style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--font-space-mono), monospace', fontSize: 13, opacity: 0.7 }}>investigations · series · data</span>

Three things live here, and they run at different speeds.

**Cases** are full investigations under the complete method: scoped, traced, evidenced, and written so someone can act on them. **THE LAST HOP** is a running public series that takes one reported scam site per episode and follows the money until it stops. **Scam Address Labels** is the dataset both produce, given away free.

Every claim carries a confidence level, no on-chain figure is published unless an explorer confirmed it, and nothing here names a person. Identities are a matter for legal process.

That extends to who holds an address. A scam site publishing a deposit address proves the site published it, not that the operator controls it, so every episode and every dataset row carries a separate **control label**. [Why that distinction exists](/kb/casework/last-hop/).

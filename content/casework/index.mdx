## The series and the dataset

Alongside full cases, [THE LAST HOP](/kb/casework/last-hop/) runs a faster public trace on a reported scam site and scores how badly the money was laundered. Every address either produces goes into [Scam Address Labels](/kb/casework/address-labels/), which is free, and which exists because vendor label coverage of small scam sites is poor and manual labels are worth something.
